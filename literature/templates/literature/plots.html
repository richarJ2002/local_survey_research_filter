How your kept papers link together

Start from your selected papers, newest first, and follow the papers they cite backwards to determine graph membership. Drawing positions prioritise clear spacing and fewer crossing lines, not fixed publication-year rows. Chronology only gives each group a loose orientation; the actual year is inside each circle. Larger circles have more citations (on a bounded scale). Disconnected groups are packed together rather than squeezed into a long strip. Hover over a line's midpoint to see who cites whom; hover or click a node to select and copy its details. Pan, zoom, or scroll horizontally when needed.
